Halfway through a task, a message draws attention elsewhere. When attention returns, the browser has another dozen open tabs.

## Give the beginning a small ritual

Pour water or make coffee. Write down the one thing this short period is for. The ritual need not be elaborate; it marks a return to the task.

## Keep one question open at a time

Not every message needs an immediate reply and not every new thought needs immediate exploration. Put interruptions on a list and advance the current question first.

## A pause belongs to the process

Focus does not require constant tension. Stand up, look outside and return. Room to recover attention can be more useful than forcing it.
