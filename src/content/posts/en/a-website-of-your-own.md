A template makes starting easier. The eventual character of a personal site comes from the choices made after that start.

## Decide what the site should express

Will it showcase work, record technical practice or collect everyday observations? Several purposes can coexist, but a visitor should recognize the site’s interests on the home page.

## Establish a consistent language

Words, colour, spacing and images all express character. A small set of shared rules creates coherence more reliably than a new effect for every page.

## Leave room for the next useful addition

The first version does not need every feature. Make articles readable and links clear. Add the parts that become useful as the content grows.
