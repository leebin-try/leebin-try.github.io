Sometimes a page has enough content and too few useful pauses.

## Spacing establishes relationships

Place a heading near its paragraph and separate unrelated articles. Distance communicates what belongs together. Useful whitespace helps readers identify structure rather than simply leaving a page empty.

## Reduce competing emphasis

When every button has an accent colour and every paragraph is bold, emphasis stops working. Identify the page’s primary action and let other elements support it.

> Restraint gives the content a chance to lead.

## Test the design through actual reading

Use a real long article: can headings be scanned, paragraphs followed and code scrolled on a phone? These details often matter more than decoration.
