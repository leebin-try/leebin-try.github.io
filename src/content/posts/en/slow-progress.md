It is easy to judge a day only by visible results and miss changes that take longer to appear.

## Give accumulation some time

Understanding one concept, clarifying one paragraph or fixing one small problem may bring no immediate feedback. These steps still form the basis for later work.

## Find a rhythm that can last

A repeatable habit can matter more than an overloaded plan. Leave a short record each day or make one small improvement each week.

> Do today’s useful work well, and let the rest take time.

## Keep a record of the process

Reading a month-old note can reveal how much has changed. A record makes quiet progress visible.
